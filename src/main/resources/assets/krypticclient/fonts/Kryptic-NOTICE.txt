Kryptic Regular
===============

Drawn for this client, not licensed from anyone. Every glyph is defined as a
set of centre-line coordinates in tools/mkfont.py and stroked into outlines at
build time by that script; the .ttf files checked in here and in ../font/ are
its output, so the script is the source and the font is the artefact.

To change a letter, edit its coordinates in tools/mkfont.py and run:

    python3 tools/mkfont.py src/main/resources/assets/krypticclient/fonts/Kryptic-Regular.ttf
    cp src/main/resources/assets/krypticclient/fonts/Kryptic-Regular.ttf \
       src/main/resources/assets/krypticclient/font/Kryptic-Regular.ttf

Both copies are needed: NanoVG reads fonts/ off the classpath directly, and
Minecraft's own text renderer reads font/ through a font provider
(font/kryptic.json). Updating one and not the other gives you a font that
changes depending on which of the two renderers happens to be drawing.
